ⓘ What is a sheet import?
Players can re-sync their Realm of Darkness (RoD) sheet after making changes there — instead of re-entering everything by hand. When they do, it lands here as a proposed sheet, compared side-by-side against what's currently live on the roster. Approving replaces the live sheet with the proposed one; denying leaves the player's current sheet untouched. This is a new feature — expect low volume for now while more players discover it.
