How to export from Realm of Darkness
  1. Go to realmofdarkness.app and open your character sheet. You must have created your character on the website — not just in Discord — for this to work.
  2. On your sheet, click the purple down-arrow button. It says Export JSON when you hover over it.
  3. Copy the full contents of the file that downloads.
  4. Paste it into the box on the left and click Import Sheet.

Your attributes, skills, disciplines, merits, and flaws will be imported. Staff can make corrections if anything looks off.
