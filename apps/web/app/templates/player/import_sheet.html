How to export from Realm of Darkness
  1. In Discord, use /sheet to pull up your character on the Realm of Darkness bot.
  2. Click the Export JSON button (download icon, bottom of the sheet).
  3. Copy the full JSON text that appears.
  4. Paste it in the box to the left and click Import Sheet.

Your attributes, skills, disciplines, merits, and flaws will be imported. Staff can make corrections if anything looks off.

One-time import. Once imported, your sheet is locked and updated only through approved XP spends. If your RoD sheet has errors, contact staff to fix them.
